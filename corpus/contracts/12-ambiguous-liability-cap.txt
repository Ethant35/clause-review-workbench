Each party that receives the other party’s Confidential Information will protect it using at least reasonable care and will use it only to perform this agreement.

Confidential Information does not include information that becomes public without breach, was already known without a duty of confidence, is independently developed without use of the information, or is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence.

A party may disclose Confidential Information when law requires it, provided it gives advance notice when legally permitted and reasonably cooperates with efforts to limit the disclosure.

This agreement begins on the effective date and continues for twelve months unless ended earlier under this agreement.

This agreement expires at the end of its stated term and does not renew automatically.

Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience on thirty days’ written notice to the other party.

Either party may terminate for the other party’s material breach if the breach remains uncured thirty days after written notice.

Each party’s liability will be limited to a reasonable amount that the parties may agree later.

The liability cap does not apply to fraud or willful misconduct.

Each party will indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent caused by its own breach of this agreement, subject to the liability cap.

Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials and intellectual property; no ownership transfers except as expressly stated in this agreement.

Customer will pay undisputed invoices within thirty days after receipt, and the fees will not change during the then-current term.

An amendment is effective only if both parties agree to it in a writing signed by their authorized representatives.

Provider will process personal data only on Customer’s documented instructions and under the data processing addendum agreed by the parties.

Provider may engage a subprocessor only with Customer’s prior written consent and will require each approved subprocessor to provide equivalent data-protection obligations.

Either party may audit the other party’s relevant records once per year on reasonable advance notice, subject to reasonable safeguards.

Oregon law governs this agreement, without regard to its conflict-of-law rules.

Neither party may assign this agreement without the other party’s prior written consent.
