# Research behind the evidence-boundary update

Author: Ethan. Checked October 22, 2026. This records source evidence and engineering decisions, not legal advice or a legal-accuracy certification.

## Astra for Law: access and evidence

**Verified official access statement:** [OpenAI's help article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001528-astra-for-law) describes initial access for selected U.S. firms, eligible lawyers, and supervised personnel. It directs requests to an account team or Sales and still describes API access as coming soon. The [legal sales form](https://openai.com/business/contact-sales-legal/) is public; submitting it is an access request, not instant access. This research did not establish a general individual, student, Pro-plan, or public API entitlement. A supervised role at an eligible firm may fit the stated category; approval is not established. No application, outreach, account change, or access attempt was made.

**Vendor measurement and professional preview claims:** [OpenAI's announcement](https://openai.com/index/astra-for-law/) reports 54.0% overall correctness versus 38.7% for general Astra with web search on 200 private validation questions from Vals AI's Legal Research Bench. This is a legal-research measurement, not contract-screening accuracy. The same announcement describes legal retrieval and instructions, and reports positive preview comments from Sullivan & Cromwell and Harvey. Those are attributed partner testimonials published by the vendor, not independent controlled trials. Its agreement-analyzer example emphasizes firm playbooks and reading provisions together. Our application does not have its legal index or claim equivalent capabilities.

**Named professional account:** [Erick Robinson's September post](https://www.ailawblog.ai/post/the-great-equalizer-astra-for-law) says his Pro 20x plan did not provide access and criticizes the rollout. His [biography](https://www.ailawblog.ai/blank) identifies him as a practicing partner. The account corroborates an access limitation for that author; it does not prove that no individual could receive an invitation. We did not adopt his broader policy argument as a product fact.

**User anecdotes:** A [self-described solo in-house lawyer](https://www.reddit.com/r/legaltech/comments/1wn8i3a/how_i_use_claude_code_and_codex_as_solo_inhouse/) reports using Codex/Claude with persistent company positions, matter folders, and tailored skills, while treating Astra for Law as a future option. In a [legaltech discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/legaltech/comments/1wkcls8/astra_for_law_the_good_bad_and_ugly/), a self-described English lawyer emphasizes jurisdiction-specific source restrictions. Identities, performance, and access were not independently verified. These reports suggest useful workflow hypotheses, not measured accuracy or public access.

## Verification method and parser evidence

[OpenAI's evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) supports task-specific cases, fixed success criteria, edge cases, and human calibration. Its [harness-aware evaluation example](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/partners/harness_aware_plugin_evals/harness_aware_plugin_evals) shows why testing isolated tools cannot establish the full deployed workflow's behavior. We therefore use real HTTP, storage, parsers, exports, and browser flows. No model score in either source is treated as a result for this app. Our synthetic expectations are agent-authored before execution, not an independent lawyer panel or a held-out legal benchmark.

[Python ElementTree documentation](https://docs.python.org/3/library/xml.etree.elementtree.html) explains that empty elements can test false. This explained a real unsupported-DOCX bypass; detection now tests tags explicitly. [python-docx's paragraph API](https://python-docx.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api/text.html) and inspected installed extraction code informed supported paragraph/hyperlink tests. [Microsoft's altChunk](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.wordprocessing.altchunk?view=openxml-3.0.1) and [content-control documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.wordprocessing.sdtcontentrun?view=openxml-3.0.1) informed real OOXML counterexamples.

[PyMuPDF extraction documentation](https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/app1.html) and [OCR guidance](https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ocr/index.html) establish that ordinary text extraction is not OCR. A real image containing terms plus a live page number reproduced incomplete extraction. The conservative fix rejects PDFs containing raster images, including otherwise benign logos, because this app cannot establish that the image contains no terms. This is an explicit compatibility cost, not OCR support. Neither this gate nor the DOCX checks certify every possible layout, hidden style, vector drawing, or external reference.

## Local outcomes and limits

The unchanged 81-check adversarial suite passed 31 checks on the original implementation and 81 after correction. It covers 24 paired semantic cases in three actual file formats, eight structural cases, and an offline network check. The original 12-contract corpus remains required. Upgrade tests run the real v1.0.0 server, create records and decisions through its API, and reopen the same database with this version. Stored history and original bytes remain intact; historical results carry a warning and must be re-uploaded before a new review.

These regressions identify specific failures. They do not establish complete legal understanding, reliable enforceability analysis, or exhaustive source completeness. More varied and independently lawyer-labeled agreements would be necessary to measure real-world accuracy. Successful AI interpretation remains BLOCKED_EXTERNAL until authorized credentials and the real live acceptance gate are available. Public Astra for Law access was not obtained, and the application does not claim it.

## Clause interactions: deeper LegalQuants review (v1.1.0)

The [SaaS issue checklist](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-skills/blob/884cf402949981ca2a4afb8ff88f19337005eecf/skills/msa-review-saas/reference/issue_checklist.md) treats the base liability limit, category exceptions, separate higher limits, and consequential-damages exclusions as distinct questions. It also connects termination rights to payment and refund effects. We adopted those dependencies, not its broad claims about what is always erroneous or commercially standard. The [perspective lens](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-skills/blob/884cf402949981ca2a4afb8ff88f19337005eecf/skills/msa-review-saas/reference/perspective_lens.md) shows why the same allocation can favor opposite sides. This app does not infer a represented party or declare a larger cap universally better.

The [playbook-review workflow](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-plugin-oss/blob/fa5a6681dc3cc9a08fa9ed48a5fd213057edafa0/skills/transactional/playbook-review/SKILL.md) calls for reading connected provisions and mapping definitions with source anchors. The [definition-check workflow](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-plugin-oss/blob/fa5a6681dc3cc9a08fa9ed48a5fd213057edafa0/skills/transactional/definition-check/SKILL.md) treats missing, repeated and inconsistent definitions as review problems. Our independent implementation links a fee-based cap to narrow or repeated Fees definitions and never silently chooses a controlling definition. These public workflows were research material, not installed or activated skills, and no upstream implementation was copied.

The implementation now adds four bounded interaction types: cap exceptions with candidate category-specific limits; narrow or repeated Fees definitions; explicit costs triggered by convenience termination; and qualified references to explicitly labeled sections. Each finding carries all identified supporting blocks, exact locations, a combined-effect explanation, and direct source navigation. Findings persist through reviewer edits and every export. Applicable checks become NEEDS_REVIEW; an existing ISSUE is not erased. The measured base cap and its scope are separate properties. Fraud/willful-misconduct-only exceptions retain the bundled baseline treatment; this is not an external legal standard.

References distinguish located targets from missing, duplicate, circular, external, unsupported and traversal-limited dependencies. Locating text is not a legal priority determination. The supported index requires explicit Section/Clause labels; bare numbering, incorporated-document completeness and general precedence remain manual. Limits are six sections/64 visits per chain, 64 evidence blocks per interaction, and 100 interactions. Oversized evidence/findings reject the review explicitly rather than store a truncated success.

The initial fixed suite reproduced false compliance in fee-cap exception/definition cases and plural references. It improved from 31/79 to 79/79 on the same 26 three-format cases plus network audit. Later edge cases exposed an unrelated breach-payment trigger and a negated reference being incorrectly linked; those were fixed with paired controls. The expanded 98-check suite covers 31 authored cases in TXT/DOCX/PDF, exact related anchors, storage, all exports, browser navigation/notes/mobile layout, resource rejection, inactive-rule isolation, and offline network behavior. These are synthetic agent-authored expectations, not independent lawyer validation or a general legal-accuracy measurement.

One original corpus expectation intentionally changed: scenario 06 now marks the base cap NEEDS_REVIEW because separate language excludes indemnity, while preserving its two objective deviations. The old expectation of unqualified cap compliance was the defect. Historical v1.0.1 reviews remain intact and visibly unverified against revision 3; their revision-2 extraction can be reused in a new review. v1.0.0 uploads still require re-upload. No legal index, new provider, automatic redlining, enforceability analysis, general defined-term solver or universal risk score was added.

The next useful extensions require more than additional keywords: party- and claim-specific allocation; indemnity defenses and exclusive remedies; hierarchy among amendments, orders and schedules; independently lawyer-labeled realistic agreements; and explicit document-package completeness. They remain outside this bounded update and should be measured against their own positive and negative controls.
